## Why PvP
- "Terror Fibercraft" = intense, competitive identity; PvP is the differentiator on an underserved Xbox CrossPlay scene.
- Raiding, base defense, and tribe wars drive engagement, clip content, and word-of-mouth.
- Retention handled by the 1000x rates (fast rebuilds after a raid) + events/leaderboards.
